## A6 — local browser/API loop

After the owner-audience/RLS defect was found, the loop also fixed the blocking
backend wiring:

- organization approval list and decision now use the owner-audience dependency;
- the decision actor comes from the owner JWT subject;
- owner-role verification uses the existing BYPASSRLS owner-status probe because
  owner JWTs intentionally have no tenant ID.

Local evidence:

| Step | Result |
|---|---:|
| Register approval request from `/register` | 202 |

designated platform owner.

session and returned 201. The legacy tenant invite widget remains a separate
UX follow-up because `/admin` is currently an owner-console path; this loop did
not expand scope by changing that routing boundary.
